About Me

From Python scripts to autonomous agents — my journey through the AI stack.

I'm a BSCS student with a deep focus on AI and software development. My progression has been deliberate and hands-on:

Python→ Data Science→ ML→ DL→ Agentic AI→ Computer Vision

Today, I build systems that combine YOLO-based detection, MediaPipe pipelines, and LLM-powered agents to solve real-world problems. I care about clean architecture, performant inference, and interfaces that feel effortless.

What drives me: the gap between a model that works in a notebook and a product that works in the wild. I've spent the last year closing that gap — optimising inference, designing responsive UIs, and shipping end-to-end AI applications.

Currently exploring: multi-agent orchestration with CrewAI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and edge deployment of vision models.

When I'm not training models, you'll find me contributing to open-source, writing about AI engineering, or experimenting with creative coding.
